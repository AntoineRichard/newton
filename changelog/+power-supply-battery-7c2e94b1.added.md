Add `newton.actuators.PowerSupplyBattery`, a stateful lithium-ion battery pack for actuators: its terminal voltage follows the open-circuit discharge curve of an NMC cell over its state of charge, minus an ohmic and a polarization drop. Regenerating motors recharge it up to `max_charge_current`, and it disconnects below `min_voltage` until reset. Create its double-buffered state with `PowerSupplyBattery.state()`.
