Add power supplies shared by the motors of several actuators: `ModelBuilder.add_power_supply()` takes a power supply class and its parameters, and `ModelBuilder.finalize()` creates one `newton.actuators.PowerSupplyBase` object per class in `Model.power_supplies`. `PowerSupplyBase.step(control, current_state, next_state, dt, next_control=None)` turns the current the motors draw, `control.actuator_supply_current`, into their supply voltage, `actuator_supply_voltage` on `next_control` or, by default, `control`, and `PowerSupplyBase.reset(control, state)` restores it. `newton.actuators.PowerSupplyDCSource` models a linear DC source with a series resistance and a parallel output capacitance. A `MotorDC` with `use_power_supply=True` reads its supply voltage from its power supply. Power supplies are authored in USD with a prim derived from `NewtonBattery`, e.g. `NewtonConstantResistanceBattery` for a `PowerSupplyDCSource`, and a `newton:battery` relationship on `NewtonActuator`, which `newton.actuators.parse_power_supply_prim()`, returning a `PowerSupplyParsed`, and `ActuatorParsed.power_supply_path` expose.
